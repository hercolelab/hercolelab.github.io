We have two appointments at **CIKM 2026** in Rome. We hope to see you there!

**Sunday, November 8, 14:00–18:30 — LLM4XAI workshop.** We will hold [LLM4XAI: Generative Models for Explainable AI Narratives](https://hercolelab.github.io/LLM4XAI/) in **Sala del Chiostro** at Sapienza University of Rome's Faculty of Engineering, San Pietro in Vincoli. The workshop brings together researchers working on reliable, useful natural-language explanations of AI decisions. See the [CIKM program](https://cikm2026.diag.uniroma1.it/program/) for the venue details.

**Wednesday, November 11, 14:00–15:30 — Paper poster.** We will present **"Counterfactual Explanations for Hypergraph Neural Networks"** at **poster A140** during Poster & Demo Session 3 in the poster area at Auditorium Parco della Musica Ennio Morricone. Come discuss how small changes to higher-order relationships can explain a hypergraph neural network's predictions. Read the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04360) or check the [CIKM program](https://cikm2026.diag.uniroma1.it/program/) for session details.
