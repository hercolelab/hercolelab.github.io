We are pleased to share that **"Counterfactual Explanations for Hypergraph Neural Networks"** by Fabiano Veglianti, Lorenzo Antonelli, and Gabriele Tolomei has been accepted at **CIKM 2026**.

Hypergraph neural networks can represent interactions among several entities at once, but it can be difficult to understand why they make a particular prediction. The paper introduces **CF-HyperGNNExplainer**, which asks what small change to a hypergraph would cause the model to change its prediction. It produces explanations by removing node–hyperedge connections or entire hyperedges, revealing which higher-order relationships mattered to the decision.

Experiments on hypergraph benchmarks show that the method generates valid, concise counterfactual explanations. Read the [paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04360).
